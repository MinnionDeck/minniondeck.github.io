Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information MinnionDeck ("we", "us") handles when you use the MinnionDeck website (minniondeck.github.io), the desktop app, its phone page and editor, and the official plugins, and what we do with it. In short: there are no accounts, no cookies and no ads, your MinnionDeck data stays on your PC, and we never sell anything about you.

1. The website

Counting visits

To see which pages are used, the website sends a small note when you open a page or follow a download or outside link. Each note has:

  • the page or link, the time, and the site you came from (only its name, like google.com);
  • your browser's language and screen width;
  • your country, and your browser, system and device type (like "Chrome, Windows, Desktop"), worked out from your connection and browser;
  • a random code that lasts only while that browser tab is open, so one visit is counted as one.

We don't use cookies, don't store your IP address, and don't build profiles of people. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. To limit abuse, our server keeps a one-way code made from your connection, browser and the day, and deletes it the next day.

Bug reports

When you send a bug report, we receive what you type in the form, the log file if you attach one (at most 2 MB), and your country, browser, system and device type. Don't put passwords or other private details in a report or log.

Plugin submissions

When you submit a plugin, we receive the form (including the creator name you give), the plugin file and any picture or profile you add, plus your country, browser, system and device type. If your plugin is approved, its name, description, files and creator name are published in the Marketplace.

Where it goes

These notes, reports and submissions pass through our small server on Cloudflare and are stored in a private repository on GitHub that only we can open. We use them only to run and improve MinnionDeck: fixing bugs, reviewing plugins and seeing which pages people use. We keep them as long as they're useful for that.

Other services the website uses

  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses, as its privacy statement explains.
  • To show the newest version, your browser asks GitHub for the latest MinnionDeck release, and downloads come from GitHub too.
  • Fonts are served from this website, not from a font service.
  • Your browser keeps a few small settings for this site (like whether the page deck is folded) in its own storage. They never leave your browser.

2. The desktop app and phone page

  • Your profiles, buttons, pictures, settings and logs are stored on your PC, in the folder you choose, encrypted with a key made for your install. We don't receive them and the app sends us no usage data.
  • The app contacts GitHub to check for updates and to show and download Marketplace plugins.
  • Phones connect to your PC directly over your own Wi-Fi, with a pairing code you confirm on the PC. Nothing goes through our servers.
  • The picture and GIF editor opens in your browser from your own PC (127.0.0.1). What you make there stays on your PC and in that browser.
  • A log file is kept on your PC to help with problems. It's only sent to us if you attach it to a bug report.

3. Official plugins

Plugins run on your PC and talk to other services directly. We don't receive what passes between them.

  • Discord connects to the Discord app on your PC. With your permission (Discord asks you to press Authorize), it can read and change your voice settings (mute, deafen, push-to-talk, voice channel) and turn your camera and screen share on or off when you press its buttons. It uses the permissions rpc, rpc.voice.read, rpc.voice.write, rpc.video.write and rpc.screenshare.write. Your Discord login is exchanged with Discord directly and kept encrypted on your PC, so you only authorize once; Disconnect in the plugin's settings forgets it. We never see your messages, servers, friends or account details. Discord's Privacy Policy also applies.
  • Spotify signs in to Spotify with your own Client ID to show what's playing and control playback. Your Spotify login is kept encrypted on your PC. Spotify's Privacy Policy also applies.
  • Weather sends the town you pick, and its coordinates, to Open-Meteo to get the forecast and air quality.
  • Timer, System Monitor and Soundboard work only on your PC.
  • Community plugins are made by other people and may handle data differently; check what each one says before you install it.
